# Grounded Ambition — deeper work, not a bigger task

Use at a meaningful planning or strategy-change boundary when it can change the result. **Do not add this procedure to every command.** Straightforward questions, formatting, translation and small fixes keep their direct fast path. Selection is advisory, not execution, acceptance or permission.

## Choose the useful axis, not all four

**Speed:** reduce time to the same verified outcome, including validation and rework. Do not optimize tool count or a local microbenchmark at the expense of correctness, Taste or the user's requested delivery.

**Altitude:** identify the outcome behind the deliverable. Preserve the original request, exact deliverable, acceptance criteria, creative intent and scope. Reframe means only inside existing authorization; a higher goal does not permit new data access, spending, publishing, continuous monitoring or taking over sessions. For an exact poem, image, interface or other creative commission, the artifact itself may be the outcome: do not replace it with a business KPI or a different product.

**Depth:** choose the professional method that closes the actual uncertainty, not expert-sounding language. Identify the rawest relevant authorized material (original source, code, logs, exact build, real scene or consented observations), a concrete method/tool, a falsifiable hypothesis and an external judge. Use the cheapest evidence adequate to reject a wrong assumption. Apply the result; more reports are not deeper work.

**Range:** recast what the work must do as verbs, then borrow only relevant techniques from other disciplines. Each added technique needs an input, method, deliverable and acceptance test. Prefer one useful extra method over a cast of personas. Additional agents are optional, require a real independent task and must fit the existing budget and resource isolation; no hidden paid model calls.

## Small decision card, only when useful

Keep these in the existing Task Spec or Work, not another mandatory planning system:
- **Outcome + protected deliverable:** what changes for the user; what must not change.
- **Evidence gap:** observed baseline and source/version; unknowns or assumptions, explicitly labeled.
- **Method:** what a leading practitioner would actually do; raw input and exact available tool.
- **Judge:** reproducible test, held-out data, actual device/provider readback or appropriate independent human review.
- **Experiment + budget:** smallest discriminating change, stretch target, resource/time/token/retry limits and next decision.

Before executing, distinguish observed facts, user reports, hypotheses and proposed tests. A tool ACK, caller-supplied evidence pointer, another model's agreement or self-score is not independent acceptance. External judges must match the claim: a build does not establish usability, pixel similarity does not prove taste, and a simulation is not real user behavior.

## Ambition with honest uncertainty

Set difficult, specific stretch targets as **hypotheses**, never guaranteed feasibility or fabricated achievements. Do not dismiss a problem just because it is longstanding or unsolved; explore new methods under the agreed budget. Failure is evidence: preserve it, revise the approach and checkpoint when resources or permissions end. No endless retry loop or lowering acceptance criteria to declare success.

For predictive findings or policy changes, freeze a historical baseline, prevent future-data leakage, validate on held-out data and measure false positives as well as wins. A shadow comparison may inform rollout; actually running it requires existing authorization and infrastructure. Do not invent telemetry, claim tests ran, or promise future monitoring.

## Reuse existing grounding and delivery

When the next observation is known, use its authorized tool directly. Use read-only `grounding_route` only when choosing the contact is genuinely unclear; it is advice, not proof. Preserve canonical Work evidence and uncertain-write/idempotency rules. Apple apps retain batch implementation -> minimum release checks -> TestFlight distribution -> full scene on that exact physical-device build; do not add per-edit test loops.

The four axes are **search directions**, not additional reporting metrics. Report exactly Speed, Accuracy/Quality and Usage Saving; treat Taste and Creativity as task-specific quality criteria. This is a harness/skill change, not model training or a claim of improved intelligence. Measure benefits on comparable completed real tasks before claiming end-to-end gains.
